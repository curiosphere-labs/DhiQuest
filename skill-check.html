Before You Begin

What you should know before taking an assessment

A few simple guidelines help keep the assessment experience fair, transparent and meaningful.

01
This is a skill assessment

The assessment evaluates your existing understanding of the selected skill. It is not a training course.

02
There are 30 questions

Each live assessment contains 30 questions carrying one mark each.

03
The passing benchmark is 70%

You need at least 21 out of 30 marks to successfully pass the assessment.

04
Read questions carefully

Some questions are designed to test practical understanding rather than simple memorisation.

05
Attempt independently

For the result to be meaningful, attempt the assessment based on your own current knowledge.

06
Your score is released after submission

Your score is made available after completing the assessment. The answer key is not displayed.

07
Passing and certification are separate

Successfully passing the assessment makes you eligible to proceed to the certificate process. A certificate is not automatically issued simply because you passed.

08
Keep your details accurate

Enter your correct name, email address and mobile number. These details are important for certificate processing.

09
Don't rush through the assessment

Take enough time to understand each question and select the answer that best represents your knowledge.

10
A lower score is not a failure of learning

If you don't meet the benchmark, use the result as a signal of where additional learning may help.

11
Retakes should be treated as fresh attempts

If you don't pass, prepare further and attempt the assessment again according to the applicable DhiQuest retake policy.

12
Understand what the certificate represents

A DhiQuest Skill Assessment Certificate verifies successful completion of a DhiQuest assessment. It does not by itself represent government, university or third-party accreditation.

Understanding Your Result

Your score tells a story.

We keep the result interpretation simple and transparent.

✓ PASS

21–30 / 30

You have achieved the DhiQuest benchmark of 70% or above for the selected assessment.

You may proceed to the certificate process for the selected skill, subject to the applicable certificate fee.

↗ NOT YET PASSED

0–20 / 30

Your current score is below the DhiQuest 70% benchmark.

Consider this a learning signal. Review the relevant concepts, strengthen your understanding and attempt the assessment again according to the applicable retake policy.
