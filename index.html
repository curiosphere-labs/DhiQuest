The idea behind DhiQuest

Awaken intellect. Pursue understanding.

The name DhiQuest brings together Dhi—intellect, discernment and thoughtful understanding—and a quest, the continuing pursuit of knowledge. Learning is more than memorising information: it also involves asking questions, examining evidence, practising skills and acting responsibly.

“धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्” — a prayer for the illumination and inspiration of the intellect.

Inspired by India's intellectual traditions and the possibilities of modern technology, DhiQuest aims to make knowledge useful, thoughtful and accessible.

धि

Dhi — Intellect

Thoughtful discernment

The ability to reflect, reason, recognise patterns and approach questions with clarity.

Quest — The pursuit

Learning in action

A continuing journey of asking better questions, testing ideas and building practical knowledge.
